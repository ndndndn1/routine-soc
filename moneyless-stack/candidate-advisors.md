# candidate-advisors.md

Authors surfaced by combo searches whose work could serve as recurring
references. Populated by task (a). Not an endorsement, just a shortlist.

## From combo-A (run 1, 2026-04-18, Routine v4)

- **George Em Karniadakis** et al. — PINN + digital twin lineage,
  engine health monitoring (arXiv:2412.11967). L1 backbone. *Carried
  forward under v6 (paper re-adopted).*
- **Vaskar Chakma, Wooyeol Choi** — 6G digital twin + edge AI for
  industrial fault detection (arXiv:2510.03807). L1/L3 bridge.
  *Demoted under v6:* the paper does not meet the v6 combo-A keyword
  threshold; still on the radar as a candidate PHYSICAL_LINKS basis.
- **Larin, Naumenko, Ivashov, Nikitin, Firsov (Fortytwo team)** —
  swarm inference with peer-ranked consensus (arXiv:2510.24801). L3.
  *Removed under v6:* not a measurement-grounded consensus paper; out
  of scope for combo-A v6 and not yet matched against combo-C v6.

## From combo-A (run 2, 2026-05-28, Routine v6)

- **Lucas Thil** + **Jesse Read** + **Rim Kaddah** + **Guillaume
  Doquet** (LIX / Polytechnique) — two adopted papers in this run
  (arXiv:2507.06672, 2511.21208) on autoencoder + RaPP latent health
  indicators with explicit aleatoric/epistemic UQ. Defines the L1
  output shape. Highest cross-paper consistency surfaced this run.
- **Waleed Razzaq, Yun-Bo Zhao** — hybrid deep-shallow RUL with
  explainability (arXiv:2510.17846). Useful counterpart to the Thil
  group: their model is interpretable-by-construction.

(Below-threshold but flagged for follow-up:)

## From combo-C (run 2, 2026-05-28, Routine v6)

- **Sophia Falk** + **David Ekchajzer** + **Sasha Luccioni** + **Aimee
  van Wynsberghe** et al. — cradle-to-grave LCA of A100 GPU
  (arXiv:2509.00093). Multi-criteria LCA *applied to AI infrastructure*
  is the closest the surveyed pool comes to a buildable L3
  measurement-input.
- **Shaina Raza** + **Graham W. Taylor** et al. — Sustainable
  Open-Source AI footprint tracking (arXiv:2601.21632). Proposes the
  ecosystem-level accounting layer that is the closest match for a
  *digital product passport* in this pool.

## From combo-D (run 2, 2026-05-28, Routine v6)

- **Pooria Roy** + **Mete Bayrak** et al. — end-to-end TinyML for
  microcontroller autonomous navigation (arXiv:2603.11071). The cheap
  L1 reference design.
- **Massimo Pavan** + **Manuel Roveri** (Politecnico di Milano) —
  TinyML speaker verification with on-device learning
  (arXiv:2406.01655). The on-device identity counterpart.
- **Runxi Huang** + **Xiaomin Ouyang** et al. — on-device multimodal
  inference scheduling (arXiv:2510.25327). The sensor-fusion
  scheduling primitive.
- **Kushal Khemani, Anjum Nazir Qureshi** — V2X-augmented predictive
  maintenance with sensor fusion (arXiv:2603.13343). The clearest
  combo-A × combo-D bridge in one artifact.
