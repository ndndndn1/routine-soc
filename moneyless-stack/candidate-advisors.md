# candidate-advisors.md

Authors surfaced by combo searches whose work could serve as recurring
references. Populated by task (a). Not an endorsement, just a shortlist.

## From combo-A — physics-input

- **George Em Karniadakis** et al. — PINN + digital-twin lineage, engine
  health monitoring (arXiv:2412.11967). L1 substrate. *Carried from
  run 1.*
- **Lucas Thil, Jesse Read, Rim Kaddah, Guillaume Doquet** — RaPP /
  C-MAPSS / UQ-for-RUL cluster (arXiv:2507.06672, arXiv:2511.21208).
  Two consecutive in-window papers; the canonical RUL+UQ recipe under
  v6.

## From combo-B — cost-surplus

- **Nataliia Roskladka, Gianmarco Bressanelli, Nicola Saccani,
  Giovanni Miragliotta** — Discover Sustainability 2024 review on
  design-for-repair for circular electronics
  (DOI:10.1007/s43621-024-00753-x). Engineering-side reference for the
  hardware repairability constraint.
- **Bonny Brandenburger, Maximilian Voigt, Simon Borgel, Magnus Busch**
  — ECIS 2024 Green IS, six-OSHW-projects case study. Information-
  systems perspective on OSHW sustainability.
- *(near-miss, not adopted but worth following)* **Giorgos Kallis,
  Jason Hickel, Daniel W. O'Neill, Tim Jackson, Peter A. Victor, Kate
  Raworth, Juliet B. Schor, Julia K. Steinberger, Diana Ürge-Vorsatz**
  — Lancet Planetary Health 2025 post-growth review
  (DOI:10.1016/S2542-5196(24)00310-3). Single-keyword match only;
  sociology background.

## From combo-C — priority-from-measurement

- **A. Gieß, F. Möller** — DPP value-ecosystem modelling
  (DOI:10.1111/jiec.13621). DPP as inter-organizational boundary
  object; ecosystem schema.
- **T. Tabata, P. Tsai** — DPP × LCA × plastic-CE
  (DOI:10.1007/s43615-025-00552-0). Maps DPP fields to LCA inputs.
- **H. Qi, Z. Dong, X. You** et al. — Extended Exergy Accounting for
  Chinese nonferrous metals (DOI:10.1038/s41598-025-29077-0).
  Operational EEA reference for the aggregation-unit primitive.

## From combo-D — near-sensor-judgment

- **Nikhil P. Ghanathe, Steve Wilton** — QUTE: TinyML uncertainty via
  early-exit ensembles (arXiv:2404.12599). Cheapest on-device UQ
  reference.
- **Runxi Huang, Mingxuan Yu, Mingyu Tsoi, Xiaomin Ouyang** — MMEdge:
  pipelined on-device multimodal inference (arXiv:2510.25327).
  Fusion-and-decide-locally pattern.
- **Ruyi Ding, Tianhong Xu, Aidong Adam Ding, Yunsi Fei** — GNNVault:
  TEE-partitioned edge GNN inference (arXiv:2502.15012). TEE-partition
  recipe.
- **Xunjie Wang, Jiacheng Shi, Zihan Zhao, Yang Yu, Zhichao Hua,
  Jinyu Gu** — TZ-LLM: Arm TrustZone for on-device LLMs
  (arXiv:2511.13717). Demonstrates commodity-ARM TEE practicality.
