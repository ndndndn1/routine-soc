# candidate-advisors.md

Authors surfaced by combo searches whose work could serve as recurring
references. Populated by task (a). Not an endorsement, just a shortlist.

- **George Em Karniadakis** et al. — PINN + digital twin lineage,
  engine health monitoring (arXiv:2412.11967). L1 backbone. *Note: v6
  re-scoring drops this paper below adoption threshold for combo-A,
  but the author's broader corpus on PINN + operator learning is still
  a candidate reference.*
- **Vaskar Chakma, Wooyeol Choi** — 6G digital twin + edge AI for
  industrial fault detection (arXiv:2510.03807). L1/L3 bridge. *Note:
  v6 drops below adoption threshold; still an engineering reference
  for the cyber-physical-twin pattern.*
- **Larin, Naumenko, Ivashov, Nikitin, Firsov (Fortytwo team)** —
  swarm inference with peer-ranked consensus (arXiv:2510.24801). L3.
  *Note: not surfaced in the v6 combo-A keyword pass, but the
  bridge-search (open work item 11) will likely re-surface this team.*

## From v6 run 1 (2026-06-04)

### combo-A — PHM with native uncertainty

- **Lucas Thil, Jesse Read, Rim Kaddah, Guillaume Doquet** (Telecom
  Paris / CEA-style lineage). Authors of two adopted papers
  (arXiv:2507.06672 and arXiv:2511.21208) on RaPP-based latent health
  indicators with aleatoric/epistemic uncertainty decomposition.
  Strongest single author group surfaced by v6 combo-A.
- **Waleed Razzaq, Yun-Bo Zhao** — CARLE hybrid deep-shallow PHM
  framework on rolling-element bearings (arXiv:2510.17846). Baseline
  reference for the non-UQ side of bearing PHM.

### combo-B — repairability / circular economy engineering side

- (Authorship from DOI 10.1007/s43621-024-00753-x, Università degli
  Studi di Brescia DfR review group, see canonical DOI page) —
  Design-for-Repair taxonomy. Useful as a stable reference for the
  *repairability_measure* schema field.

### combo-C — measurement substrate

- **Alessandro Pracucci, Matteo Giovanardi** (Levery S.r.l.) —
  Sensor-Based Digital Product Passport for low-tech manufacturing
  (MDPI Sensors 25(18) 5653). The pragmatic-DPP author group; their
  later work is worth watching as the DPP schema firms up.
- **H. Qi, Z. Dong, X. You et al.** — Extended exergy accounting at
  industry scale (Sci Rep 15:44816). The thermodynamic-numeraire
  lineage; useful for node-local exergy budget design even though
  this specific paper is macro-scale.

### combo-D — near-sensor judgment

- **Pooria Roy, Nourhan Jadallah, Tomer Lapid, Shahzaib Ahmad,
  Armita Afroushe, Mete Bayrak** (TinyNav, arXiv:2603.11071) —
  end-to-end TinyML on commodity MCUs. Useful as the "what fits
  on an ESP32" reference.
- **Ruyi Ding, Tianhong Xu, Aidong Adam Ding, Yunsi Fei** (GNNVault,
  arXiv:2502.15012) — TEE-on-edge for ML inference. Useful as the
  "what crosses the trust boundary" reference.
